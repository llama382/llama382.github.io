# Participants 
On a given night there will be 1d4 visitors willing to gamble. Each participant carries 1d10+10 GP.
## Exhaustion 
There is a 1/6 chance after a loss that a visitor decides to stop gambling and hold onto some of their money.

A visitor will also leave the table once they have doubled their initial amount of GP.
# Games
## Goblin 17
Legend says this game was created when a goblin sailor tried to gamble his way out of walking the plank. Nowadays it's the most popular game played across the [[Material Plane]].

The rules are simple, each player roles 2d10 and tries to get to a total of 17. They may choose to re-roll one of their dice, but must be careful, as getting a total above 17 leads to an automatic loss.
# Cheating 
Participants can try to cheat during any game by using magic, slight of hand, or other skills at their disposal.
