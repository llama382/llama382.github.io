# Introduction
The Gold for XP system is a method of giving players the option of saving up their gold to level up their character. 

What players like to spend their PC’s money on will vary from person to person. Some love to spend as quickly as they earn, buffing their PCs via upgraded items and weapons. Others, who may be more attached to their character’s gear and style, may be unsure what they’d like to spend their gold on. This system grants the latter category the option of investing in their own character’s stats. 

In the context of the [[Walking Marketplace]], the Master Sessions are personal, one-to-one classes with an esteemed practitioner of combat. Base the lecturer’s teaching style on the PC’s class and combat style. For example, a ranger doesn’t have to be trained by just another ranger. They could be taught how to focus their concentration in combat by a monk, how to ‘feel’ the battlefield by a forest spirit, or even lessons in battlefield maneuvering by a retired military commander.
# How to use
The Gold for XP system is designed to be flexible. If you are running a straightforward dungeon crawl and want to keep players focused on combat and tactics, you can use the ‘Unknown’ values below to calculate an individual player’s reward share, giving them the option of either investing in better equipment or leveling up their character’s stats. 

For the more story or roleplay-based campaign, we have a few suggestions that will help make this system more integrated into your world-building. We recommend having your PC actively seek out an NPC who will conduct this training: this will helps to allow GMs to further flesh out the world whilst making the level-up all the more satisfying for the player. A fighter may seek lessons from a veteran living in self-imposed exile, a rogue may want the insight of a Legendary assassin currently being prepared for execution, or a wizard may begrudgingly make an appointment to visit the Director of Dark Magics - the next slot being available within the following 7 months… 

Regardless of the knowledge they seek, for the best roleplaying experience, have your players go beyond just paying the gold cost. We have developed three tiers of prices for levels, Unknown, Familiar, and Trusted. This allows you, as the GM, to incorporate roleplaying perks into the cost of leveling up (in a balanced way) whilst also encouraging players to see these NPCs as not just plot devices, but living, breathing people.

# Training Mishaps! Rolling Table
Sometimes intense training doesn’t go so well, so here are a few ideas on fun ways your players can suffer issues! You could even choose to incorporate a CON-save check to see if they successfully avoid injury altogether.

| D6  | Event                                                                                                                     |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Forgot to protect the face during a boxing session, -1 to CHR checks for the next week while you heal.                    |
| 2   | You got a bit cocky in training and your mouth made some bets your body couldn’t keep up with! Lose 100gp                 |
| 3   | You are getting stronger! Shame your weapon wasn’t built to withstand it! Destroy 1 non-magical weapon in your equipment. |
| 4   | Twisted your ankle and went face-first into the mat! -1 to DEX checks for a week while you try and recover your dignity.  |
| 5   | Your trousers couldn’t quite survive your latest set of squats… Now rumors swirl around the marketplace.                  |
| 6   | No, your leg should not bend that way. -5 ft from your movement speed for the next 3 days.                                |
